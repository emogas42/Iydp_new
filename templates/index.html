About us

About IYDP

The International Youth Dialogue Platform (IYDP) is a Public Union founded in Baku, Azerbaijan, in 2019. The organization is committed to empowering young people through international experiences, non-formal education, and opportunities for personal and professional growth. By promoting intercultural dialogue, volunteering, and youth mobility, IYDP supports the development of active and globally engaged citizens.

IYDP actively participates in international programs such as Erasmus+ and the European Solidarity Corps (ESC), fostering collaboration, learning, and solidarity among youth. Since 2020, the organization has held the ESC Quality Label, ensuring high-quality volunteering and educational opportunities that contribute to youth development and cross-cultural exchange.

Through its initiatives, IYDP promotes intercultural understanding, leadership, and employability, while building strong international partnerships. The organization implements both local and international projects that encourage dialogue, innovation, and sustainable development, supporting young people in gaining essential skills and global perspectives.

With a focus on youth development, education, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion, IYDP continues to create impactful opportunities for young people-helping them grow, connect, and contribute to a more inclusive and peaceful world.

Key Objectives

Increase opportunities for youth to participate in international projects and programs.

Implement initiatives that promote intercultural dialogue and cooperation.

Develop youth as active and responsible individuals capable of responding to global challenges.

Support knowledge and skills development through non-formal education methods.

Increase social integration of youth and support their role as active representatives of Azerbaijan in the international arena.

Erasmus+ & ESC

European Solidarity Corps

IYDP is an accredited sending and hosting organization under the European Solidarity Corps program, holding the ESC Quality Label since 2020. This recognition ensures our volunteering and youth exchange activities meet the highest European standards.

Through Erasmus+, we run youth exchanges, training courses, and capacity-building projects across Europe and beyond — opening doors for Azerbaijani youth to the world.
